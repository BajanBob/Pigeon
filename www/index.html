Share my location — live & encrypted

Make a private link and send it to one person; they just tap Accept to see you on the map. You choose whether to add a live video or audio feed, and you can turn them off or stop sharing whenever you want. Everything goes phone-to-phone, end-to-end AES-256 encrypted — the relay only helps the two phones find each other and can't read anything.

Privacy: pick a My location mode above — GPS shares your exact spot, IP shares your network/VPN location, Masked lets you navigate exactly but shares a ~1 km-fuzzed position (hidden from the other person, area still known). Hide my IP keeps WebRTC from leaking your real IP.

⚙️ Advanced: TURN server & phone settings

Relay-only needs a working TURN server. metered.ca (Open Relay) gives a free tier (~50 GB/month): sign up at metered.ca → create an app → copy your turn: URL, username & credential → paste them here. Without your own, the app falls back to a shared free TURN that can be slow or down.

  • VPN: connect your VPN app (e.g. NordVPN) — the app then follows that route.
  • Private DNS: Android → Settings → Network & internet → Private DNS → “Provider hostname” (e.g. one.one.one.one or dns.nextdns.io).
  • Location permission: keep it allowed — GPS needs it; Masked still shares only a fuzzed point.

Tap Video feed or Audio feed to include them — before or during a share. You can turn each off or stop sharing entirely at any time.
